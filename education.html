Bachelor's, Master's and PhD

Bachelor's study may suit applicants progressing from secondary education. Master's study builds on prior university learning; doctoral study is shaped by research interests and postgraduate preparation. Institutions set their own academic, language and admission requirements.

HOW THE PROCESS WORKS

A clear journey, with the right conversation at each stage.

The first enquiry is free. If you are ready and suitable to proceed, the initial consultation and assessment are paid services; any further support is agreed separately.

  1. 01

    Initial enquiry

    Tell us what you hope to study and where you are considering. There is no charge to send an initial enquiry.

  2. 02

    Enquiry review and qualification

    An administrator reviews your enquiry and contacts you to understand your plans and readiness.

  3. 03

    Client onboarding

    If both sides are ready to proceed, the team can create your client record and arrange Client Portal access.

  4. 04

    Initial consultation and assessment

    This is a paid service. It begins after the required payment is verified.

  5. 05

    Detailed assessment and preparation

    The team reviews your background and discusses appropriate preparation under the agreed service scope.

  6. 06

    Document collection and review

    When needed, documents are requested through the authenticated Client Portal and reviewed by the team.

  7. 07

    Application planning

    Suitable opportunities and application steps are considered based on research and your agreed support.

  8. 08

    Admission and subsequent preparation

    Institutions make admission decisions. Any next preparation is discussed according to the outcome and agreed scope.

  9. 09

    Travel and arrival support

    Where applicable and agreed, the team can discuss practical preparation for travel and arrival.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Education FAQs

Requirements and availability differ by programme, institution, destination and applicant. We can help identify what needs to be checked.

Can LORDBLESS arrange admission to a university?

We can help research options and support applications where an appropriate service is agreed. Admission decisions belong to each institution and cannot be guaranteed.

Can I apply for a Bachelor's, Master's or PhD?

Start with a free enquiry and tell us about your interests. If you are ready to proceed, detailed assessment is part of the paid consultation and assessment service. Institution requirements vary.

What is Germany Ausbildung?

Ausbildung is a vocational training route, distinct from a university degree. The applicable occupation, provider criteria, language expectations and other requirements need specific verification.

What is the difference between Ausbildung and a degree?

Ausbildung is vocational training, with occupational and practical preparation, rather than a university degree. Nursing Ausbildung and healthcare routes have their own entry and language considerations which need individual checking.

When does the paid assessment begin?

The initial enquiry is free. The initial consultation and assessment are paid services and begin after the required payment is verified during client onboarding.

What academic qualifications do I need?

Requirements vary by course and institution. The paid consultation and assessment can help identify what needs to be checked; institutions determine eligibility.

Do I need a language test?

Language and examination requirements depend on the programme and destination. Mention tests you have completed or plan to take in your initial enquiry.

When will I need to provide documents?

Do not send documents with your initial enquiry. After onboarding, the team can request documents through the authenticated Client Portal.

How much does consultancy support cost?

Fees and service scope are provided in an individual written quotation after we understand your needs. No fixed price is published on this page.

Can you find scholarships or funding?

Funding research can be discussed as part of a proposed service. Availability and eligibility change, and funding decisions belong to the relevant provider.

How long does an application take?

Timelines vary by programme, institution, intake and the applicant's preparation. A suitable timeframe can be discussed after the intended route is clearer.

Can LORDBLESS guarantee a visa?

No. LORDBLESS can provide preparation guidance where agreed, but only the relevant authorities decide visa applications.

Can you help with travel, arrival and settlement?

Travel, pre-departure and destination support may be discussed as separate needs. Scope and availability are confirmed in the written proposal.
