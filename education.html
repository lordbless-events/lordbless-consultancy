COMMON QUESTIONS

Education FAQs

We are here to help you take informed next steps in your international education journey.

Can LORDBLESS help me apply to a university?

Yes. We help you explore suitable programmes and prepare applications aligned with your qualifications and goals.

Can I apply for a Bachelor's, Master's or PhD?

Yes. We support applicants exploring undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral study options.

Which study destinations can I explore?

Explore Germany, Canada, the USA, China, Romania, Serbia, Poland and other destinations through our Education enquiry service.

What is Germany Ausbildung?

Ausbildung is a vocational training route combining occupational learning and practical preparation.

Can I study Nursing or pursue healthcare education abroad?

Yes. Nursing and healthcare education options vary by destination and provider. We help you explore a pathway aligned with your background.

What academic qualifications do I need?

Entry requirements depend on the programme and institution. We help you identify the qualifications relevant to your chosen pathway.

Do I need to speak the local language?

Language expectations vary by destination and programme. We can help you understand the preparation relevant to your plans.

What documents are needed for an application?

Document requirements vary by institution and programme. We guide you through collecting and preparing the documents relevant to your application.

Can LORDBLESS help me find scholarship opportunities?

We help you explore available funding opportunities and understand the relevant eligibility requirements.

How long does an education application take?

Timelines vary by programme, destination and intake. We help you plan around the requirements and deadlines that apply to your goals.

Can LORDBLESS help me attend an international conference?

Yes. We help individuals and organisations identify relevant international conferences and professional learning opportunities, prepare registration documents and organise visa and travel preparation where required.

Can you help with travel and settlement preparation?

We support relevant travel, arrival and settlement arrangements as part of your education journey.
